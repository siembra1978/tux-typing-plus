# Swedish typing exercises

Select a word set marked “Swedish” in Fish Cascade, Comet Zap or Proper Rhythm.
Phrase Typing offers both English and Swedish poems. In PenguinType mode, use
the word-set menu to switch between the Swedish 100-, 1,000- and 10,000-word
sets. A Swedish locale starts with the 100-word set.

## Word sets

Short, medium and long words follow Swedish word lengths. The frequency lists
use the [Stockholm–Umeå Corpus word statistics](../README.md#swedish-penguintype-word-sets).

The themed sets cover the same subjects as the English exercises. Some draw on
Swedish teaching vocabulary. The finger drills use all three letter rows of a
Swedish keyboard, including Å, Ä and Ö.

- The animal, astronomy, fruit, food and number sets adapt the Swedish animal, space, fruit, vegetable and number exercises from [KDE educational vocabulary data](https://github.com/KDE/kdeedu-data/tree/73c46cd3794d754713c06a658dd681e88d621ef8/data/kvtml/sv), under [GPL version 2](../LICENSE). Spelling has been updated and duplicate entries removed.
- The Swedish finger assignments come from [Tux Typing’s Swedish keyboard](https://github.com/tux4kids/tuxtype/blob/fa9fc6ce86e7de0cb7eb43bd87201b5d130dd9ec/data/themes/svenska/keyboard.lst), under [GPL version 2](../LICENSE).
- Swedish element names and mathematical terms follow the [libqalculate data](https://github.com/Qalculate/libqalculate/tree/master/data), under [GPL version 2 or later](https://github.com/Qalculate/libqalculate/blob/master/libqalculate/Calculator.cc#L5-L10).
- Colour and continent names use [Unicode CLDR 48 Swedish locale data](https://github.com/unicode-org/cldr/tree/release-48/common). The names were selected and adapted for typing practice. The [Unicode license and copyright notice](licenses/Unicode-3.0.txt) apply to that material.
- Ocean names use the Swedish labels from [Natural Earth’s public domain marine geography data](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_geography_marine_polys.geojson).
- The Arctic animal exercise features wildlife found on Svalbard. [Norwegian Polar Institute species guide](https://npolar.no/en/themes/fauna-svalbard/).
